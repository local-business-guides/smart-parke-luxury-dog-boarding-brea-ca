Prepare a boarding handoff checklist

Gather the information the facility requests before packing supplies. Confirm its current health documentation requirements, food instructions, and rules for personal belongings. Check whether containers need labels and whether bedding or toys are accepted. Bring only approved items so the handoff stays straightforward.

Your written care outline can include:

  • Meal portions, feeding times, and food storage instructions.
  • Known allergies or sensitivities that affect daily care.
  • Veterinary contact information and an emergency contact.
  • Behaviors staff should understand before handling your dog.
  • Any medication questions that need an answer before reservation.

If your dog takes medication, ask whether the facility accepts the specific care involved and what documentation it needs. Do not assume that bringing a bottle is enough. For questions about whether boarding is appropriate for a health condition, consult your veterinarian before finalizing arrangements.

Avoid gaps in your Brea boarding instructions

One avoidable mistake is giving different directions verbally and in writing. Review your notes before arrival so meal amounts, contact details, and special requests agree. Another is withholding a difficult behavior because you worry it might affect acceptance. Accurate information helps the facility assess whether the proposed stay is suitable.

Ask how you should report changes after booking. A new medication, a recent illness, or a significant behavior change may require another discussion. Keep your emergency contact informed about the dates and their role, rather than listing someone who does not know they may receive a call.
