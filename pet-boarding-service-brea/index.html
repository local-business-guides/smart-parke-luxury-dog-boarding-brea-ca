Prepare a clear boarding handoff

Create a concise care sheet that someone can read without needing to interpret household shorthand. Separate everyday instructions from issues that need a phone call. Check the facility's requirements before packing items or sending records.

Your preparation list can include:

  • Food name, portion size, and usual meal times.

  • Medication details to discuss before booking.
  • Your contact information and an alternate contact.
  • Veterinary contact details requested by the provider.
  • Notes about handling, rest, and unfamiliar surroundings.
  • A list of belongings the facility agrees to accept.

Prevent confusion during pet boarding

Avoid bringing an unfamiliar food simply because it is easier to pack. Tell the provider what your dog normally eats and ask how supplies should be labeled and measured. Any planned dietary change should be discussed before the stay rather than introduced unexpectedly at drop-off.
