Feeding details for a boarding stay

Write feeding instructions as if someone has never met your dog. Include the food name, portion size, usual meal times, and any restrictions on treats. Avoid directions such as “a normal scoop” unless you also identify the scoop and explain the amount.

Ask whether food should arrive in individually labeled portions or its original container. Confirm how leftovers are handled and whether bowls are provided. If your dog sometimes refuses meals in unfamiliar places, describe that history and ask how the facility communicates concerns.

Medication needs require a separate discussion. Explain what your dog takes and ask whether the facility can accommodate the instructions. Do not assume every boarding provider offers the same medication support.

A packing checklist for dog boarding yorba linda

Confirm the facility's current requirements before gathering supplies. A compact checklist can keep important information from getting lost among bags and bedding:

  • Reservation dates and an agreed pickup time.
  • The records or forms the facility requests.
  • Written meal instructions and approved food packaging.
  • Medication details, if relevant, discussed in advance.
  • Your contact information and an alternate contact.
  • Veterinary contact details and care authorization instructions.
  • Only the comfort items the facility permits.

Label accepted belongings with your dog's name. Photograph what you pack so you can check the contents at pickup. Keep instructions together rather than distributing different versions across messages, containers, and handwritten notes.
